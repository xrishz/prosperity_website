## Stack

## Users

Prospective travelers browsing destinations and contacting a Philippine travel agency, commonly using a mobile phone. The supplied brief includes families, couples, friends, corporate groups, students and people seeking travel documentation assistance.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors discover destinations, understand the agency's services and start an inquiry with a human travel consultant. The client's questionnaire explicitly chooses destinations followed by inquiries.

Confirmed services: domestic tours, international tours, airline ticketing, hotel reservations, visa assistance, passport assistance, cruises, customized/private tours, corporate/group travel and travel insurance.
